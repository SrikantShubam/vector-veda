## Overview

**Web Design Maestrro Finplan Academy** * 2023

MaestroFinplan Academy is a leading institution in the financial planning industry, renowned for providing comprehensive coaching for the Certified Financial Planner (CFP) certification and facilitating successful career placements for its students. The Academy holds a prestigious status as an Authorized Education Provider, officially recognized by the Financial Planning Standards Board (FPSB).

## Role

**Business Analyst**

## Responsibilities

- Competitor Appraisal and Strategic Intelligence
- Digital Marketing

## Team

- Sushipra Sharma, Product Manager

## Problem

The Academy faces a significant challenge with its limited online presence, hindering its ability to provide affordable, high-quality financial education to a wider audience. This issue affects its potential for increased profitability, necessitating effective strategies for enhanced online visibility and accessibility.

- Poorly designed webpage with no popular CTA
- No organic marketing resulting in poor organic growth
- Basic website with no expenditure on marketing resulting in lowering of margins in which conversion costs are very high

During the course of my case study analysis, a pertinent observation came to light within the identified market segment. This specific sector appears to be inundated with numerous small-scale players who predominantly conduct their operations through offline channels, with their online presence being largely symbolic in nature. This limited online engagement has had a notable impact on their ability to disseminate content and subsequently impairs their potential for substantial growth.

Furthermore, it became apparent that these entities, grappling with substantial overhead costs associated with their offline operations, often encountered formidable challenges in attracting prospective students to their educational institutes. This predicament frequently translated into margin erosion during various quarters of their operation.

## Our Vision

A mere website overhaul won't suffice in an industry where users are reluctant to pay high online fees. Instead, our website should be transformed to provide substantial value through various means, leaving a lasting impact that can reduce conversion costs in the long run. This approach focuses on user-centric experiences, engagement, and value creation to build trust and loyalty.

## Features

### Webinars

Implementing the Webinar Section has been a game-changer for MaestroFinplan Academy's platform. The real-time sessions led by industry experts foster active user engagement and provide a tactile preview of our educational depth. This feature has proven instrumental in boosting our conversion rates, as users, post-webinar, transition from mere interest to concrete enrollments. The immediacy and accessibility of these webinars have become pivotal touchpoints in our user's journey, transforming potential students into committed learners.

### FAQs

The FAQ Section on Maestrro Finplan Academy's platform serves as a proactive problem-solving hub. By addressing common queries and concerns, we've streamlined user navigation and decision-making. This dedicated space not only reduces barriers for potential students by eliminating uncertainties but also demonstrates our commitment to transparency and user-centric design. Its presence ensures users spend less time in doubt and more time engaging with our core offerings.

### Research & Testimonials

In our research, we discovered that most of the testimonials in this sector were either fake or lacked credibility. According to a study by BrightLocal, 91% of consumers are more likely to use a business that has positive reviews, and 84% trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations.

Recognizing the profound impact of authenticity on consumer behaviour, we integrated LinkedIn IDs of our alumni and previous users alongside their testimonials on the MaestroFinplan Academy platform. This strategic move not only lends verifiable credibility to each review but also bolsters trust among potential students. This transparent approach has effectively differentiated us in the market, leading to heightened consumer confidence and increased conversions.

**Testimonial Section Components:**

1. LinkedIn Button - taking users directly to the testimonial provider giving the users personal verification and enabling trust in the brand
2. The actual testimonial as provided by the user specifically mentioning the name of the institute, the course and the Mentor for increasing SEO reach
3. The name of the testimonial provider along with a hyperlink that takes users to the LinkedIn account of the testimonial provider
4. The job position as well as the name of the company the testimonial provider works at
5. The image of the testimonial provider

In enhancing the FAQ and Testimonials sections, I iterated through diverse mockups, each tailored for optimal user engagement and clarity. The final versions—a succinct FAQ design for swift information retrieval and an inviting Testimonials layout—embody my focus on user-centric simplicity and design flexibility.

## Results

Incorporating an authentic testimonials section, where genuine feedback from verified LinkedIn accounts took center stage, our approach yielded noteworthy results. The conversion of over 20 users stands as a testament to the real impact of these testimonials, with the added credibility of being linked to verifiable LinkedIn profiles—making the experiences shared even more compelling.

This authentic touch had a ripple effect, leading to a significant surge of **612 seminar requests**. This surge not only highlights the persuasive power of genuine user experiences but also underscores the practical utility of LinkedIn as a platform for building trust and fostering engagement within our target audience.

Over a 30-day period, our dedicated LinkedIn page consistently drew an average of **45 users per day**, maintaining a robust figure even during off-session durations. This sustained engagement speaks to the enduring appeal and reliability of the platform in connecting with our audience, thereby amplifying the impact of our testimonials.

## Overview

**Frontend Food Court Web Based App** * 2022

The presence of over three major food vendors within the University, alongside numerous external food vendors catering to students' dietary needs, has inadvertently led to financial losses and a decrease in customers for the vendors serving inside the campus. This surplus of food options outside the university has diverted students' attention and patronage away from the campus vendors, impacting their profitability and customer base negatively.

## Role

**Lead Front End Developer**

## Responsibilities

- Frontend Development
- UI/UX Design
- Testing and Debugging

## Team

- Bal Gangadhar, Professor
- Nilay Purayar, Junior UI/UX Designer

## Problem

The vendors within the University face a dilemma: the reluctance to introduce new menu items due to potential financial risks has resulted in a static food selection. This cautious approach has led to a lack of variety, leaving students disinterested in the available offerings. As a consequence, the dining experience has become less enticing, failing to meet the evolving tastes and preferences of the student body.

The food served in the Campus

### Research Findings

Amidst our investigation at the University, a significant challenge surfaced: vendors grappled with a static menu, lacking insights into students' culinary preferences. This dearth of understanding hindered menu innovation, leaving it stagnant.

1. **Vendor Insights Gap:** Research uncovered a significant challenge - vendors faced difficulty in gathering insights about the food preferences of students. This lack of understanding directly contributed to the static nature of the menu offerings.

2. **Absence of Student Input Platform:** Another critical finding highlighted the absence of a democratic platform where students could collectively contribute and democratically decide on the food choices available. This absence of interaction between students and vendors limited the opportunity for students to express their preferences effectively.

## Our Solution

Our solution entailed creating a web application where vendors access a dashboard to post upcoming dishes, while students, using their student ID, can log in to vote on the offerings. This platform fosters interactive communication, enabling students to democratically influence menu choices based on their preferences.

## Design Methodology

I adopted a focused approach by crafting a minimalistic theme rooted in two distinct yet interconnected keywords: 'food' and 'court.' This design endeavor demanded a strategic synthesis of elements associated with gastronomy and legal proceedings within a minimalist framework. Through a deliberate fusion of typography, color schemes, and an uncluttered layout, I aimed to evoke a sense of sophistication while ensuring a seamless user experience.

## Key Features

### The Leaderboard

One of the pivotal features of our web app was the implementation of a dynamic leaderboard, showcasing an array of dishes. This feature served as a focal point for users, offering a comprehensive view of available options.

A strategic color scheme of green and red was thoughtfully incorporated, leveraging simplicity to swiftly assist users in distinguishing between vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. This intuitive visual cue not only simplified navigation but also enhanced user experience, allowing for quick and effortless identification of preferred food choices within the app.

**Leaderboard Components:**

1. Presenting the date prominently, with a special emphasis on highlighting days
2. The image and the current position of the menu items
3. The name of the menu item and a small triangle helping the user distinguish between vegan and non vegan offerings
4. Basic filters placed at a prominent location allowing the Users to choose between the different categories of menu items
5. The name and the ingredients present in the menu item
6. A small triangle showcasing the users between vegan and non vegan options and just below the current votes the menu items has received
7. Menu allowing the users easier access
8. UI animation for easier transition of menu items

## Overview

**Data Scientist WebSite Classification Web App** * 2023

The project aims to automate website content categorization using machine learning techniques. It focuses on creating a model that analyzes textual and visual content, assigning them to relevant categories. By employing NLP and computer vision, this solution seeks to enhance user experience, facilitating efficient content discovery and navigation across diverse web domains and topics.

## Role

**Data Scientist**

## Responsibilities

- Model Training
- Front End
- Dev Ops

## Team

- Vishal Bimal Francis, Senior Data Scientist

## Problem Statement

The challenge at hand involves creating an intelligent system adept at precisely categorizing a wide array of website content into appropriate categories. Manual categorization methods are often imprecise and time-intensive, impeding efficient content discovery for users. Given the potential deceitful nature of website names, this project offers a vital tool for users to vet websites.

### Research Context

Studies and reports from reputable organizations such as Pew Research Center and RAND Corporation underscore the pervasive influence of misleading websites and disinformation campaigns in shaping public opinion, particularly during critical societal debates and elections.

Highlighted by journals like JAMA Network Open, the impact of health misinformation propagated by deceptive online sources is a significant concern, influencing individuals' decisions regarding medical treatments and preventive measures.

Moreover, cybersecurity firms like McAfee, Kaspersky, and Symantec consistently report on the prevalence of phishing attacks, often orchestrated through misleading websites, illustrating how users are tricked into divulging personal or financial information due to deceptive online tactics.

In parallel, research from institutions like MIT and Oxford delves into the spread of false news on social media platforms and its consequential impact on public perception and behaviors. These comprehensive investigations emphasize the need for vigilance against misleading websites that perpetuate false narratives, showcasing the far-reaching consequences of deceptive online content.

While my information is current up until January 2022, ongoing research and studies continue to underscore the critical role of misleading websites and fake content in influencing public opinion, exacerbating health-related concerns, facilitating financial scams, and shaping societal behavior in the digital realm.

## Our Solution

The objective is to devise an automated solution utilizing machine learning and NLP techniques to accurately categorize website content based on its textual and visual elements. By doing so, this system aims to streamline content navigation, ensuring enhanced user accessibility and trustworthiness across diverse web domains.

### Data Collection and Preprocessing

In the data collection phase, I acquired a comprehensive dataset encompassing around **1378 unique websites**. To assemble this dataset, I utilized web scraping techniques to extract textual information from various web pages across these sites. Web scraping involves programmatically fetching and parsing website data to gather relevant content for analysis.

Following the data collection process, I initiated the data preprocessing stage, a crucial step in preparing the dataset for analysis. During preprocessing, I employed foundational Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to refine the raw textual data. These techniques included:

- POS tagging
- Stemming
- Stop words and punctuation removal
- Tokenization

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Key metrics analyzed:
- Total number of URLs divided per category
- Total number of characters in each category
- Most common words per category

## Model Building and Evaluation

To select the most fitting model for the classification task, I evaluated four diverse algorithms using **5-fold cross-validation**, prioritizing accuracy as the primary evaluation metric:

1. **Random Forest**
2. **LinearSVC**
3. **Multinomial Naive Bayes**
4. **Gaussian Naive Bayes**

### Results

After performing 5-fold cross-validation on these four algorithms, the analysis revealed that **LinearSVC exhibited the highest mean accuracy across folds**, coupled with the lowest standard deviation among the models. This consistency indicated a narrower range of variation in performance, showcasing a more stable and reliable accuracy rate.

Therefore, considering both the highest mean accuracy and the minimal fluctuation in performance among folds, I opted for the **LinearSVC algorithm** as it demonstrated the most consistent and promising classification performance for the dataset.

## Conclusion

The selection of the LinearSVC algorithm was based on its superior performance in accurately classifying website content categories, offering a reliable and consistent approach for categorization tasks on diverse web domains. The model's robust accuracy, combined with its stability during cross-validation, underscores its suitability for this specific dataset and highlights its potential for real-world deployment in similar classification endeavors.
