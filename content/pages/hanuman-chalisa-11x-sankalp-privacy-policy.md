## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

- **Device and app information:** device type, OS version, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
- **Usage data:** app interactions such as feature usage, session activity, and engagement events.
- **Advertising data:** ad requests and related identifiers provided by advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob), as permitted by your device and regional settings.
- **User-provided content:** limited in-app values needed for app functionality (for example, progress, preferences, language, reminders, and devotional counters).
- **Sacred Thread/Bhakt Mandal data (if used):** room or participation data you create/share in that feature.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless explicitly required for a feature and disclosed in-app.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use data to:

- provide and improve app features,
- maintain app stability and performance,
- analyze usage trends,
- send local reminders/notifications (if enabled),
- show ads and measure ad performance,
- prevent abuse and maintain service integrity.

## 5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app may send scheduled devotional notifications. You can disable notifications anytime in device settings.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

Some data is stored locally on your device (such as app preferences and progress). Cloud-backed feature data, if any, is retained only as needed for functionality, analytics, legal compliance, and security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us to request deletion.
