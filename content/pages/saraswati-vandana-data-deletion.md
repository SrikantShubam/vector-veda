**Last updated:** March 7, 2026

If you want your data deleted from Saraswati Vandana, contact us at:
**[support@vectorveda.in](mailto:support@vectorveda.in)**

**Subject line:** "Delete My Data - Saraswati Vandana"

Please include:

- Your in-app display name (if used in Vandana Circle)
- Room code (if applicable)
- Device/platform (Android)
- Any details that help us identify your data

## What we delete

- Vandana Circle cloud data linked to your in-app identity (where identifiable)
- App-linked data we control in backend systems

## What may be retained

- Data required for legal/security/fraud-prevention purposes
- Aggregated or de-identified analytics/crash data that cannot reasonably identify you
- Temporary backups, removed per normal backup cycle

## Processing time

We process deletion requests within 30 days.

## If you only want to stop local app data

Uninstalling the app clears most on-device app data.
